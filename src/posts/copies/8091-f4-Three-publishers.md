Mounted or bound-in are:-- ephemera relating to this copy (inside front cover).--ms. notes by Paton (on front pastedown and on front flyleaves or on leaves tipped thereonto).--photoreproduction of the title page of Plutarch, Lives of the Noble Grecians and Romaines Compared, London, 1612 (front flyleaf verso), annotated by Paton (on the flyleaf verso below the photoreproduction). Paton treats the Plutarch (1612), specifically the copy the title page of which is pictured in the photoreproduction (supposedly Shakespeare’s own, then in the Greenock Library) in his Introduction to his edition of Cor. Haughton.  Pub. June 4, 1801, by J. & J. Boydell at the Shakespeare Gallery. ...", stipple engraving.--ephemera relating to Isabella Dallas Glyn.
