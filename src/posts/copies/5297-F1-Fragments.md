A very early, if not its first, owner was Josias Dawson of Halton Gill, who married Mary Foster of Langcliffe Hall in 1642 (Josias's elder son Christopher was at Cambridge with Isaac Newton, who is said to have visited him on his estate later in life). Josias's great-grandson Pudsey Dawson (1752-1816) was mayor of Liverpool in 1779 and 1780. Josias's great-great-grandson Pudsey Dawson II (1778-1859) was a founding Director of the North West Railway. He sold Langcliffe Hall in the early 1830s to the Swale family of Settle, but the estate was re-acquired in 1860 by Miss Elizabeth Hutton Dawson of Marshfield, Settle. Geoffrey Dawson (nee Robinson, b.1874), who inherited the estate in 1917, was the editor of The Times from 1910 to 1942 (with a short break between 1919 and 1922)."
