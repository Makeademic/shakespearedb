Copy Details for Troilus and Cressida (1609)

Ohio State University

A collection of loose leaves from various copies, associated with the initial leaves found in SC 5324, including King John pp 3-4, 5-6; Tempest 5-6, 9-10; Coriolanus 5-6, 11-12, 21-2, 27-8; Titus 41-2 (water stain on outer bottom), 51-2; 1H4 51-2, 69-70, 71-2; 2H4 79-80 (water stain on outer margin), 81-2, 83-4, 85-6, 91-2 (leaf gg2), 91-2 (leaf gg3), H5 89-90, 91-2, 93-4; 1H6 99-118; 2H6 125-30, 133-6, 139-40, 145-6; 3H6 159-60, 167-72 (red wax drop on inner column of 167; ink smudges on 172); H8 215 [217]-222 (worm holes throughout), 225-28; R3 177-8, 181-4 (continuous wormhole indicates from same copy), 189-90 (same copy as previous), 191-2 (no wormhole but a tear in that spot), 193-6 (same wormhole); Errors 93-4 (inner margin repaired with paper with mathematical calculation and some illegiible text in early hand), 97-8 (same repair as previous but no MS); TN 261-2, 273-[276]; WT 297-8 (repaired around header), 299-300; Ham 261-2; AYL 187-8 (leaf Q4), 187-8 (leaf Q5), 189-98; Cymbeline 371-8 (still conjugate), 389 [379]-86 (still conjugate); RJ 61-2, 69-72; T&C sigs ¶2, ¶4-6, ¶¶1, ¶¶3-4 (still conjugate), ¶¶6 (inner margin repaired with same waste paper as in Errors, with "Numer[ator]" and "[De]nominator" visible and mathematical calculations). The leaves included are sigs. A3, A5, H5, I1, Q4-R3, Y5, Z5-6, Bb5-6, a2-3, e1, f4-5, g3-6, [superscript chi]gg2-3, i5-k1, k4-m1, m5-n1, n3-4, n6, o3, p4, q1, q3-4, r1, r3-4, s1-4, v2, v4-5, x1-2, ¶2, ¶4-6, ¶¶1, ¶¶3-4, ¶¶6, aa3, aa6, bb5, cc2, dd3, ee2, ff1, ff5-6, oo3, zz4-aaa5. Sigs. zz4-aaa5 (Cymbeline) are from Folger copy 80 (SC 5111).
