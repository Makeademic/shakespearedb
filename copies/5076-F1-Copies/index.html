Copy Details for Comedies, Histories, and Tragedies: F1 (1623)

Walters Art Museum

In this copy the crossbar of the "f" in "fold" in the imprint has begun to break off. Portrait heavily offset onto "To the Reader" page. From auction catalogue: "To the Reader" leaf with lower outer blank comer renewed and some repairs to blank gutter margin, minor renewal to blank gutter margin of title leaf, both of these probably supplied, some small holes to 10 leaves sometimes affecting text (d1, el, s6, Bb6, ee2, gg3, hh4, kk1, kk3, ss3), marginal tears on S3 (repaired) and 14, e3-f6 supplied and with extreme lower edges neatly remargined, some staining to upper portion of approximately 75 leaves ([cc6]-ff6, hh6-ii6, nn6-pp6, tt6-vv6, bbb6-ddd4), extreme lower outer blank comers of approximately 140 leaves gnawed, scattered spotting"

Notes about issue [This edition exists in three distinct states: the first includes copies that were sold without Troilus and Cressida; the second contains Troilus and Cressida but without its prologue and with a redundant final page of Romeo and Juliet crossed out by the printer; the third includes a cancel leaf with the Prologue of Troilus on its recto and the first page of the play, reprinted from a new setting of type, on its verso. In none of the states is Troilus included in the "Catalogue" of plays.]
