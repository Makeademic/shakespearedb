Copy Details for 1&2 The Troublesome Reign of King John (1611)

British Library

Various leaves, not necessarily from the same original copy, numbered in purple pencil from 1 to 72 in order of binding; 2 copies of most included leaves bound back-to-back to display variants on same page in same opening: Errors pp. 89-90 (sig. H3; purple pencil on first p 90 next to 'E.Ant. I thinke thou art an asse', though this line does not vary from other p. 90), Much Ado pp. 111-12 (sig. K2; pencil circling an inked blank on first p. 112, instead of correctly inked italic 'B' in 'Beatrice' ), Shrew pp. 213-14 (sig. S5; incorrect pagination '212' circled on second leaf), Shrew pp. 225-6 (sig. T5; purple check mark on 225 next to 'Welcome, one messe is like to be your cheere' which is poorly inked in other leaf ), Shrew / All's Well pp. 229-30 (sig. V1; signature mark 'V' circled on one leaf, which is 'Vv' on other), All's Well pp 237-8 (sig. V5; pagination '239', with 9 deleted and 7 written in ink, circled in pencil), R2 pp. 37-8 (sig. d1; pagination '39' circled on one leaf and '37' circled on the other), 2H6 pp 121-2 (sig. m3; incorrect signature mark 'l3' circled on one leaf; second copy of this leaf is damaged at fore-edge, with pen facsimile of the outer rule), 3H6 pp 153-4 (sig. p1, one copy only; probably included due to poorly inked SD on 154a); 3H6 pp 163-4 (sig. p6, one copy only; unclear why included), 3H6 pp. 171-2 (sig. q4, one copy only; MS at top 'King Henry the Sixth'; purple pencil marks next to 'Clarence and Gloster, loue my louely Queene' and 'And kis your Princely Nephew Brothers both', H8 pp. 217-8 (sig. v3; signature mark on one copy circled, does not appear on other copy), Troilus unpag. (sig. ¶¶2; last line verso of one copy marked with an 'X', because in other copy a blank has inked after the semicolon), Cor pp 21-2 (sig. bb5, one copy only; unclear why included), RJ pp 57-8 (sig. ee5; red mark on one leaf next to 'oft athe angry Mab', also with underlining in pencil, where other copy has 'oft the angry Mab' with shoulder inking of 'M'), RJ pp 59-60 (sig. ee6; 'thy' on one copy circled and marked on other copy with red underlining and 'x' where it reads 'this'), Ham pp 277-8 (sig. pp5; incorrect pagination '273' with '3' deleted and '7' written in ink, but not marked; two words on each copy marked with 'x' and circled: 'iowlos' and 'fices one' on one copy and 'iowles' and 'fices:one' on the other), Lear 289-90 (sig. qq5, one copy damaged at fore-edge with pen facsimile of outer rule; incorrect catchword 'Gainst' marked with 'x' in one copy, correct catchword 'But' circled and marked with 'x' in the other), Lear pp. 307-3[0]8 (sig. ss2; catchword 'A' circled and marked with 'x' in one copy, where in the other copy it is missing), A&C pp. 349-50 (sig. xx5; one copy lacks pagination, but this is not marked; catchword 'Rise' marked with 'x' and circled in one copy, where in the other the type has fallen)
