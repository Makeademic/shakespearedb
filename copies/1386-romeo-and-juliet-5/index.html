Binding The binding is calfskin with gold tooled and blind tooled ornamentation around 'SAMUEL PARKER | BOSTON' embossed on a red leather piece. There are five raised bands with gold tooled ornamentation on the first, third, fourth, and sixth panels. 'SHAKESPEARE' is in the red second panel. 'ANNO. 1623.' is in the red fifth panel. Ornamented portions of boards and spine are pasted on top of brown leather. All edges are red. This folio is stored in a brown clamshell box.
Binder
Marginalia The first two pages of Henry VIII are missing and there are six extra leaves bound into this volume with the missing text hand written on them. There is evidence that someone worked up Julius Caesar for performance and marked scene divisions.
