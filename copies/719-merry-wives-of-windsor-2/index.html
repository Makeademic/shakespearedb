Copy-specific Notes
Provenance Capell, Edward (1713-1781)
Provenance notes Halliwell, J. O. James Orchard (1820-1889): see Euing's acquisition note on verso of front free endpaper. A bibliographical note in Halliwell's hand on front flyleaf: "Sotheby's 23 May 1856 £4..6..0" - this seems to refer to a different copy since the sale catalogue, "A catalogue of a very valuable and important collection of Shaksperian [sic] and dramatic literature...chiefly consisting of the books used for the first five volumes of Mr Halliwell's folio edition of Shakespeare; which will be sold by auction by ... S. Leigh Sotheby and John Wilkinson ... at ... 3. Wellington St., Strand, on ... May 23d, 1856..." (London: Sotheby's 1856), describes the listed copy of the 1634 'The two noble kinsmen' (lot 331) as "morocco by Bedford".\r\nWilliam Euing (1788-1874), insurance broker, Glasgow: acquired from Halliwell 17 October 1856. \r\nUniversity of Glasgow: Euing bequest, 1874.
