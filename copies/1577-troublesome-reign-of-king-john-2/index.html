Copy Details for 1&2 The Troublesome Reign of King John (1611)

University of Delaware

Catalogue leaf (sig. πA6), Tempest first leaf (sig. A1), Measure pp. 83-4 (sig. G6), King John first leaf (sig. a1); Catalogue leaf heavily damaged and crudely repaired and backed; Tempest leaf has slashes where acid of ink has eaten through paper; ink splotch on ornament at end of Measure; King John leaf smaller than others and trimmed close to the bottom rule; piece of tape adhered to top and bottom of King John leaf; with loose inserts of "The First Folio Shakspere" by A.W. Pollard, from the Sphere magazine section on the Tercentenary, 20 May 1923, and a photograph of a First Folio opened to title page with note on back: "The Most Beautiful Book in the World for Sale" about sale of Daniel copy at Burdett-Coutts auction (now Folger copy 5, SC 5036).
