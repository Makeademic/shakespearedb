Copy Details for Comedies, Histories, and Tragedies: F1 (1623)

National Library of New Zealand

Hay Star copy: Imperfect: lacks leaves πA1-3; leaves πA2,3 (title, dedication) supplied from the 1807 E. and J. Wright type-facsimile reprint of the First Folio; title red-ruled, with "The second Impression" in ms. above the portrait, and the date altered to 1632 (though the anomalous Jaggard imprint is left unaltered); the plate has cut through the paper at some points. Leaf πA5 (conjugate with the missing title leaf), containing "Vpon the effigies of my worthy friend ...", etc., is in Todd's state Ib, indicating that the book was included in the first issue, though the original imprint of this copy, and its corresponding STC and ESTC entries, are indeterminate--see Todd. Leaves N3.4 (p. 149-152) are trimmed shorter at top and bottom, indicating that they may have been supplied from another copy.\r\n\tIn this copy, the signatures are as given by Greg for all copies, including none of his parenthesized variants except for the missigning of 2c2 as 2b2 and the omission of a signature on 2c3. (Note also that v1,2 are signed u1,2.) The pagination errors are as indicated for all copies, including none of the parenthesized variants.

Notes about issue [This edition exists in three distinct states: the first includes copies that were sold without Troilus and Cressida; the second contains Troilus and Cressida but without its prologue and with a redundant final page of Romeo and Juliet crossed out by the printer; the third includes a cancel leaf with the Prologue of Troilus on its recto and the first page of the play, reprinted from a new setting of type, on its verso. In none of the states is Troilus included in the "Catalogue" of plays.]

Copy-specific Notes
Provenance Kerslake, Thomas (1812–1891), Hawkins, Charles Halford (1838–1900)
Provenance notes Gift of William V.A. and Georgiana MacArthur Hansen in 1972, together with a copy of the Fourth Folio and the second quarto of Julius Caesar; the Hansens had already donated, in 1971, a copy of the First Folio (cataloged as Hay Rare PR2751.A1x 1623). All three folios had previously been owned by Mrs. Hansen's father, Alfred MacArthur. See the Josiah record for the First Folio for further notes on the Hansens' connection to Brown University.

Binding Bound in early 19th-c. calf (presumably ca. 1808 or later, given the 1807 title leaf), rebacked; covers blind-tooled to a panel design, using small tools and floral roll tools; spine blind-tooled, lettered direct in early 19th-c. face, "Shakespeares comedies histories and tragedies / Second impression / Lond. 1632"; binder's endleaves are handmade laid, with fleur-de-lis/LVG watermark; marbled endleaves (simple comb pattern); all edges red.
Binder
Marginalia A few early marginal x's in the text; otherwise without annotation except for the margin of p. 179 (P6r), which contains a number of alternate spellings of words from the text (e.g. "doe" for "do", "mercie" for "mercy") in an early italic hand, lightly trimmed.
