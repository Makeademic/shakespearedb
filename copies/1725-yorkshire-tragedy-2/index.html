Copy Details for A Yorkshire Tragedy (1619)

John Rylands Library, University of Manchester

From auction catalogue: "Verses leaf offset from portrait [i.e., the portrait and letterpress portion of the title page has offset onto the verses leaf], lightly foxed, and with a marginal stain and pen trial at deep inner margin, title-page lightly stained at deep inner margin, evidently from facing pen trial, a tiny wormhole running through the lower margin of A1–2Aa4, not affecting text and with early mend to first 8 leaves, very short marginal tear to lower corner of zz4, about 20 scattered rustholes costing a letter or two, some scattered soiling and staining."
