Copy Details for Richard III (1634)

Eton College

Imperfect: wanting the two unsigned preliminary leaves, with verses by Digges and I.M., and names of the actors. The preliminary verses "To the reader" are inlaid on a leaf which is red-ruled on recto and verso; other preliminary leaves are hinged. Inserted after binder's leaves at front: single sheet, printed on verso only, being facsimiles of Shakespeare's signature from various documents; source unknown. (Another copy at this location, Rasmussen & West # 218, has been missing since 1972, when it was stolen.)

Copy-specific Notes
Provenance C. A. Stonehill, Inc
Provenance notes Acquired ca. 1744 by Martin Folkes, F.R.S. (1690-1754). Sold at Folkes's sale Feb 21, 1756, by Samuel Baker, for £3 3s. to Dr. John Monro (1715-1791), an associate of George Steevens (1736-1800). It seems likely that Monro left this copy to Steevens, from whom it subsequently passed to the second Earl Spencer, probably ca. 1790. It was certainly in Earl Spencer's library before 1822, when it was catalogued by T.F. Dibdin, Earl Spencer's librarian at Althrop.
